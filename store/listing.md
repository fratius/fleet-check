## English

**Subtitle** (Apple, 30): `Van handovers & damage checks`

**Short description** (Google, 80): `Van handovers, damage checks and the history of every van, for delivery fleets.`

**Promotional text** (Apple, 170):
`Know who had which van, when, and what it looked like. Photo or video handovers, AI damage checks and the van history in one app for owners, managers and drivers.`

**Keywords** (Apple, 100): `fleet,van,handover,damage,inspection,walkaround,delivery,driver,vehicle check,fuel,mileage,Flotte`

**Description** (both, 4000):

```
Fratius keeps track of a delivery fleet: who has which van, what the van looked like when they took it, and what changed by the time they brought it back.

HANDOVERS WITH PROOF
Drivers pick up and drop off vans with a short video or a guided set of photos. Every handover is recorded for good: the van, the driver, the time, the kilometres. Fleet managers can hand vans out themselves, and see straight away when a pick-up video is missing.

DAMAGE CHECKS
The camera guides the driver around the van. The AI marks spots that could be damage and asks for close-ups; the fleet manager has the final word on each one. Approved damage goes on the van's record with when it happened and who had the van in that time, with the before and after photos.

THE HISTORY OF EVERY VAN AND DRIVER
Which drivers had a van, and from what kilometre to what kilometre. Which vans a driver had. Who was driving at a given time, for fines and complaints that arrive days later.

TOURS, FUEL AND WASHES
Where the company turns it on, drivers photograph the dashboard at the start and end of a tour: kilometres, fuel and warning lights are read for them. Refuels and washes are checked against the van's fuel card and receipt, and the manager is asked when something doesn't fit.

CHECKS FOR DRIVERS
Oil, tyres, charging cable, a clean cargo area: fleet managers set what drivers check on their van and how often. The driver ticks it off or reports a problem with a photo; the manager is reminded when it isn't done.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
In a garage or a car park with no reception, handovers, photos and videos wait on the phone and go through by themselves once there's a connection. Nobody has to do anything twice.

FOR OWNERS, MANAGERS AND DRIVERS
Fleet owners see every station; fleet managers run their own; drivers see their van and their own record. Drivers and managers join through one-time invite links. The app speaks 32 languages, so every driver can use it in their own.

PRIVACY
Photos and videos of a month are deleted on the 15th of the month after, never while the van is still with its driver; the ones that show damage after 90 days. Card photos and document photos are deleted right after reading. No ads, no tracking.

Any delivery company can start right away: create your company in the app, or open the demo company with made-up vans and people to look around first. More at fratius.com.

Fratius is an independent product, not affiliated with Amazon.
```

## Deutsch

**Untertitel** (Apple, 30): `Fahrzeugübergabe & Schäden`

**Kurzbeschreibung** (Google, 80): `Übergaben, Schadenschecks und die Historie jedes Transporters für Lieferflotten`

**Werbetext** (Apple, 170):
`Wer hatte wann welchen Transporter, und wie sah er aus? Übergaben per Foto oder Video, KI-Schadenscheck und die Historie jedes Fahrzeugs in einer App.`

**Schlüsselwörter** (Apple, 100): `Flotte,Transporter,Übergabe,Schaden,Fahrzeugcheck,Fuhrpark,Fahrer,Tankkarte,Kilometer,Fleet`

**Beschreibung**:

```
Fratius behält den Überblick über eine Lieferflotte: wer welchen Transporter hat, wie er bei der Übernahme aussah und was sich bis zur Rückgabe verändert hat.

ÜBERGABEN MIT NACHWEIS
Fahrer übernehmen und übergeben Transporter mit einem kurzen Video oder einer geführten Fotoserie. Jede Übergabe wird dauerhaft festgehalten: Fahrzeug, Fahrer, Uhrzeit, Kilometerstand. Flottenmanager können Fahrzeuge auch selbst zuteilen und sehen sofort, wenn ein Übernahme-Video fehlt.

SCHADENSCHECKS
Die Kamera führt den Fahrer einmal um das Fahrzeug. Die KI markiert Stellen, die ein Schaden sein könnten, und bittet um Nahaufnahmen; das letzte Wort hat der Flottenmanager. Bestätigte Schäden kommen in die Schadenshistorie des Fahrzeugs, mit dem Zeitraum und den Fahrern, die es in dieser Zeit hatten, und mit den Fotos davor und danach.

DIE HISTORIE JEDES FAHRZEUGS UND FAHRERS
Welche Fahrer ein Fahrzeug hatten, von welchem bis zu welchem Kilometerstand. Welche Fahrzeuge ein Fahrer hatte. Wer zu einer bestimmten Zeit gefahren ist, für Bußgelder und Beschwerden, die Tage später kommen.

TOUREN, TANKEN UND WÄSCHE
Wenn das Unternehmen es einschaltet, fotografieren Fahrer das Armaturenbrett zu Beginn und am Ende einer Tour: Kilometer, Tankstand und Warnleuchten werden für sie abgelesen. Tanken und Waschen werden mit der Tankkarte des Fahrzeugs und dem Beleg abgeglichen; passt etwas nicht, wird der Manager gefragt.

KONTROLLEN FÜR FAHRER
Öl, Reifen, Ladekabel, ein sauberer Laderaum: Flottenmanager legen fest, was Fahrer an ihrem Transporter prüfen und wie oft. Der Fahrer hakt es ab oder meldet ein Problem mit Foto; der Manager wird erinnert, wenn es nicht erledigt ist.

FUNKTIONIERT OHNE EMPFANG
In der Halle oder auf dem Parkplatz ohne Netz warten Übergaben, Fotos und Videos auf dem Handy und gehen von selbst raus, sobald wieder Verbindung da ist. Niemand muss etwas doppelt machen.

FÜR INHABER, MANAGER UND FAHRER
Inhaber sehen alle Stationen, Flottenmanager ihre eigene, Fahrer ihr Fahrzeug und ihre eigenen Daten. Fahrer und Manager kommen über einmalige Einladungslinks dazu. Die App spricht 32 Sprachen, damit jeder Fahrer sie in seiner eigenen nutzen kann.

DATENSCHUTZ
Fotos und Videos eines Monats werden am 15. des Folgemonats gelöscht, nie solange der Fahrer den Transporter noch hat; solche mit Schäden nach 90 Tagen. Fotos von Tankkarten und Dokumenten werden direkt nach dem Auslesen gelöscht. Keine Werbung, kein Tracking.

Jedes Lieferunternehmen kann sofort loslegen: das eigene Unternehmen in der App anlegen oder zuerst das Demo-Unternehmen mit erfundenen Fahrzeugen und Personen ansehen. Mehr auf fratius.com.

Fratius ist ein unabhängiges Produkt und nicht mit Amazon verbunden.
```

## Review notes (Apple "App Review Information", Google "App access")

Make a review login first (APP-STORE.md, step 5): sign up in the app or the web app with an address such as
`review@fratius.com` and a password of your choice, and tap **Open a demo company**. Put the email and password in
both stores.

```
Fratius is a fleet app for delivery companies of any size: fleet owners, fleet managers and their drivers. It isn't made for one particular company. Any delivery company can create an account, create its company in the app and start using it; drivers and managers then join their company through one-time invite links from it. There are no in-app purchases and the app shows no prices or purchase links. Companies that keep using it after the trial pay us under a business contract for their organisation (guideline 3.1.3(c)); nothing is sold to individual consumers.

Sign in with the login below. It is the fleet owner of a demo company with made-up stations, people, vans, handovers and damage checks; its photos are drawings.
- To see the app as a fleet manager or driver: Account tab > Demo company shows two more logins and their passwords.
- You can also create a new account on the sign-in screen and tap "Open a demo company", or "Create a company" to start an empty one.
- Account deletion: Account tab > Delete account (at the bottom). Deleting this login also deletes its demo company; a new one is made by signing up again and opening a demo company.

Camera: walk-around photos and videos of the vans (videos are recorded without sound), photos of the dashboard, fuel card and receipts. Notifications: Account tab > Notifications (for example when a manager gives a driver a van). The app works without a connection: changes wait on the phone and are sent when it's back.
AI: photos for damage checks, dashboards, fuel cards, receipts and documents are analysed by Anthropic's Claude API; a person confirms every result.
```

## Apple App Privacy (App Store Connect > App Privacy)

Tracking: **No**. For every type below: **linked to the user**, **not used for tracking**, purpose
**App Functionality** only.

| Category | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info | Name | profile, who did what |
| Contact Info | Email Address | login |
| Contact Info | Phone Number | mobile number in the company |
| User Content | Photos or Videos | walk-arounds, handovers, damage, dashboard, receipts |
| User Content | Other User Content | comments, notes, incidents |
| Identifiers | User ID | the account |
| Financial Info | Other Financial Info | last 4 digits of the company fuel card |
| Purchases | Purchase History | refuels, washes and parts bought for the company's vans |
| Other Data | Other Data Types | driving licence classes and checks, employment dates, kilometres and fuel per tour |

Not collected: location, audio (videos are recorded without sound), health, browsing or search history, usage data, diagnostics, contacts, sensitive info.
