This policy covers the website fratius.com, signing up in the web app, and customer accounts and their payments. What the Fratius app processes once you use it is in the app's own privacy policy.

2. Opening the website

The website is hosted on Firebase Hosting, a service of Google. When you open a page, your browser sends data that the server processes automatically: your IP address, the date and time, the page you asked for, the page you came from, and your browser and operating system.

We need this to deliver the website and to keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Google may process data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and Google's data processing terms also include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We don't combine this data with other data.

3. No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The fonts are stored on our own server, so opening the website sends no data to font providers.

4. The request form ("Start your 30 days")

When you send the form, we receive: the plan you picked, your company's name and country, how many stations and vans you run, your first and last name, your work email and your mobile number, and whether you want to hear when AI damage detection is ready.

We use this to answer your request and to set up your trial. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If you tick "Tell me when AI damage detection is ready", we email you about it once it's ready; the legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by writing to us.

Your request is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (part of Firebase), on servers in the USA, and a copy is sent to our mailbox info@fratius.com. To stop automated spam, the server keeps a one-way code made from your IP address for up to 24 hours; it doesn't store the IP address itself.

We delete your request once it's dealt with, at the latest 12 months after our last contact, unless it leads to a contract. Then the retention periods for business records apply.

You don't have to send the form, but we can't answer a request without a way to reach you.

5. Signing up in the web app

You create your company in the web app at app.fratius.com. We receive your email address and a password, your name, your company's name and its first station, the plan and billing period you picked, where you signed up (for example from a plan on this website or through a personal sign-up link), and which version of the terms (AGB) and the data processing agreement (AVV) was accepted, when and by whom. Firebase Authentication stores the password only as a protected hash; we can't read it.

We use this to create your account and your company, to run the free trial and the contract, and to be able to show which terms were agreed. The legal basis is the contract and the steps before it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for keeping proof of the accepted terms, also our legitimate interest in being able to prove the contract (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The data is stored in Google Firebase (part of Google Cloud), on servers in the USA. Google processes it on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR), with the safeguards for transfers to the USA described in section 2. When you sign up you also enter a card for the payments; see section 6. How the app processes data once you use it is described in the app's privacy policy.

We keep your account until you delete it, and your company's data for as long as the contract runs and as set out in the terms (§ 15 AGB). We keep the record of the accepted terms for as long as we may need it to prove the contract, usually until claims under it are time-barred (three years from the end of the year in which the contract ended).

6. Customer accounts and payments

When your company uses Fratius as a customer, we process for the contract and its payments: your company's name, the name and email address of the person who signed up or handles billing, the billing address, your VAT ID if you give it, the plan, billing period and add-ons, and the payment and invoice history (amounts, dates, invoices, whether a payment went through).

You enter your card details directly with Stripe: the card form in the web app (Stripe Elements) comes from Stripe and sends the card details straight to Stripe. We never receive the full card number or the security code; Stripe tells us only details such as the type of card, its last four digits and its expiry date. Apple Pay and Google Pay work the same way.

Stripe may process data in the USA and in other countries. Stripe, LLC (USA) is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and Stripe's data processing terms also include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

We keep invoices and accounting records for as long as tax and commercial law require, which is 8 to 10 years (§ 147 AO, § 257 HGB). We delete the other customer and payment data once the contract has ended and the data is no longer needed for it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

The data needed for the contract and the payment is required; without it we can't provide Fratius as a paid service.

7. Email

When you write to us, we use your message and your contact details to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for questions about a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our mailbox is provided by Google Workspace, a service of Google, which processes these emails on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

8. Links to other services

The website links to the Fratius web app and, once they're available, to the App Store and Google Play. Their own privacy policies apply when you open them.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • have wrong data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • have the processing restricted (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • receive your data in a common format (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • withdraw a consent at any time, for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

Write to info@fratius.com. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work (Art. 77 GDPR).

10. No automated decisions

We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing. Stripe checks payments automatically to prevent fraud (see section 6).
