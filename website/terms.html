Annex 2 – Service description

1. Functions

All plans include the same functions, for any number of stations and drivers:

  • roles: fleet owner, fleet manager (per station) and driver, with rights by role; drivers join with the customer's driver link and the approval of the fleet owner or a fleet manager of their station, fleet managers through one-time invitation links that expire after 24 hours;
  • vehicle handover at pick-up and return with photos or a video as proof, by drivers only where the station allows it;
  • walk-arounds with photos and videos; review of possible damage by the fleet manager or fleet owner (approve or reject) and a damage list per vehicle;
  • an ongoing history of who had which vehicle when and with what mileage;
  • tours based on dashboard photos, if the customer switches them on;
  • refuels, washes and parts with a fuel card check and a receipt photo;
  • driver records, incidents, vehicle records and deadlines with reminders;
  • reading mileage, fuel level and warning lights, fuel cards, receipts, vehicle type and document photos with AI (§ 5);
  • checks for drivers;
  • push notifications in the web application and in the apps;
  • capture without an internet connection with later synchronisation;
  • data tables with export as an Excel file; downloading videos.

Not part of the plans: AI damage detection (in preparation, § 5(4)), an application programming interface (API) for customers, and an availability commitment (§ 4(1)).

2. System requirements

The web application runs in a current browser (e.g. the current version of Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox). The apps require a current version of iOS or Android; the minimum version they need is shown in the App Store or on Google Play. Photos and videos need a device with a camera, synchronisation needs an internet connection.

3. Retention and deletion rules

  • Photos and videos of a month are deleted on the 15th of the following month (kept 15 to 45 days), but not while the vehicle is still with the driver who took it over; they are then deleted on the 15th of the month after the vehicle was returned.
  • Photos and videos marked as damage (damage approved by the fleet manager, or recordings the fleet owner or fleet manager marked as damage) are deleted on the 15th following the 90th day after they were taken.
  • Photos of fuel cards and documents are deleted right after reading; of the fuel card only the last four digits are stored.
  • The records themselves (handovers, walk-arounds, damage checks, tours, refuels, washes and parts, driver and vehicle records, incidents, checks) are kept for the term of the contract; after the end of the contract, § 15 applies.

4. Export and formats

Exportable within the meaning of § 15 are the following data:

  • by the customer in the app as an Excel file (.xlsx), for a chosen period: the data tables drivers, tours, vehicles, and refuels, washes and parts (fleet owners for all stations, fleet managers for their station; costs only for fleet owners);
  • by the customer in the app: videos as MP4 files to download (fleet owners and fleet managers), as long as they have not been deleted under no. 3;
  • by the customer in the browser or phone: photos (JPEG) with its save function, as long as they have not been deleted under no. 3;
  • on request in text form, by Fratius in a common machine-readable format (e.g. JSON or Excel): stations and members, vehicles and vehicle records, handovers and vehicle history, walk-arounds, damage checks with decisions, damage lists, tours, refuels, washes and parts, driver records, incidents, deadlines and checks.

Not exportable are data used only for the internal operation of the Service: technical logs, internal identifiers and security data, and the duration and cost of AI analyses. Data for which Fratius itself is the controller (user accounts, billing) is governed by the privacy policies.
